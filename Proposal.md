## PROJECT PROPOSAL
### [3D Clothing Simulation in E-Commerce]
**Group:** Nakpum

#### 1 Project Overview
This project focuses on developing a 3D clothing simulation feature for e-commerce platforms. The prototype aims to provide a virtual try-on experience that allows users to preview how clothes fit, match colors, and look on a 3D avatar, thereby bridging the gap between physical and online shopping.

#### 2 Problem Statement
Many online clothing buyers struggle with uncertainty regarding the fit, size, and color matching of garments before purchasing. This lack of physical interaction leads to low customer confidence, high return rates, and a less engaging shopping experience. Current e-commerce platforms often rely on static images which do not accurately represent how a garment will look on different body types.

#### 3 Objectives
The goal objectives are:
*   Increase customer trust and confidence in online purchases.
*   Reduce product return rates due to sizing and fit issues.
*   Enhance user engagement and attractiveness of online clothing stores.
*   Increase overall sales and conversion rates for e-commerce businesses.

#### 4 Proposed Solution
This project introduces a 3D simulation system integrated into e-commerce websites/apps that helps users preview and try on clothes virtually.
*   **Target users:** Online shoppers and e-commerce business owners.
*   **Platform:** Web or Mobile App integration.
*   **Main workflow:**
    *   **User Profile/Avatar Creation:** Users input their measurements or select a 3D avatar that closely matches their body type.
    *   **Select Clothing:** Users browse the store and select items they wish to try on.
    *   **3D Simulation:** The system renders the selected clothing on the avatar, allowing the user to rotate and view the fit from different angles.
    *   **Customization:** Users can quickly swap colors or sizes to see which combination suits them best.
    *   **Purchase:** Once satisfied with the virtual fit, the user can proceed to checkout with higher confidence.

#### 5 Development Timeline
*   **Week 1:** Planning and content selection (Defining 3D model requirements and UI/UX flow).
*   **Week 2 and 3:** Development (3D engine integration and simulation logic).
*   **Week 4:** Testing and demo (Refining simulations and final presentation).
