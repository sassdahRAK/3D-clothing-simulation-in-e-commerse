PROJECT PROPOSAL
[VR in Chemistry lab]
[Group: V-09]
1  Project Overview
Provide a brief introduction to the project and explain what the prototype aims to achieve.
This project focuses on developing VR in chemistry simulation (experiment) that is designed specifically for high school students in grade 9. The prototype purpose is to build safe and 3D laboratory environment that allows all students to conduct curriculum-mapping experiments such as reaction of acid-base and color change.

2  Problem Statement
Describe the existing problem or challenge that motivates the development (such as current situation, existing limitations, user impact).
Some schools lack the budget to purchase expensive lab equipment, and many students got physical accident from reaction of experiment. Furthermore, some students aren’t interested in theory so it can lead them to lack of knowledge to prepare for next grade so that why we must use VR to attract them that can make them focus more.

3  Objectives
State the goals of the project.
The goal objectives are:
•	Create safety learning
•	Improve academic engagement 
•	Reduce cost

4  Proposed Solution
Provide a high-level description of the project including overall concept, target users, platform, main workflow.
This project introduces VR in chemistry lab that is designed to make high school chemistry experiment safe, interactive and highly visual.
Target users: For high school students from grade 9 to grade 12.
Platform: VR headset
Main workflow:
•	Select module: The student wears the headset and chooses an experiment corresponding to their grade level from the main menu.
•	Interactive lab: Guided by a virtual assistant, the student uses VR hand controllers to measure, pour, and mix chemical compounds in virtual beakers.
•	Microscope view: When a reaction occurs, the student triggers a "zoom" mode to watch the atoms collide and form chemical bonds at a molecular scale.
•	Assessment : Upon completing the lab, the student takes a quick interactive quiz to test their understanding of the experiment's results.



5  Development Timeline
Outline the timeline for each phase.
•	Week 1: Planning and content selection
•	Week 2 and 3: Development
•	Week 4: Testing and demo

Ministry of education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS), Chemistry Textbook.
