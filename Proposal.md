## PROJECT PROPOSAL
### 3D Clothing Simulation for E-Commerce
**Group:** Nakpum

#### 1. Executive Summary
This project proposes a 3D clothing simulation platform that helps online shoppers preview clothing on a digital avatar before purchase. For shoppers, it reduces uncertainty about fit, size, and style. For e-commerce businesses, it increases buyer confidence, lowers return rates, and improves conversion rates.

The product will be offered as a web-based service that can be integrated into an existing e-commerce store or used as a standalone shopping experience.

#### 2. Business Problem
Online fashion shopping has one major weakness: customers cannot try products physically. This creates three common problems:
* Customers are unsure whether the item will fit their body.
* Customers cannot easily judge style, color, and appearance from static images.
* Businesses lose money from abandoned carts, product returns, and low customer trust.

This problem affects both small and large sellers, especially fashion brands that depend on visual appeal and fit accuracy.

#### 3. Why This Solution Matters
Online shoppers and e-commerce owners should invest in this platform because it solves a real and costly problem.

For shoppers:
* They can see how clothes may look on a body shape similar to their own.
* They can compare sizes and colors before buying.
* They gain more confidence when placing an order.

For business owners:
* They can improve sales by reducing purchase hesitation.
* They can reduce return costs caused by wrong size expectations.
* They can make their store more competitive and modern.

#### 4. Target Customers
The platform will serve two main customer groups:

1. **Online shoppers**
   * Regular fashion buyers who want a better shopping experience.
   * New buyers who are uncertain about fit and style.
   * Repeat customers who want faster decision-making.

2. **E-commerce business owners**
   * **Start-up stores:** businesses that do not yet have their own website or want a ready-made fashion platform.
   * **Small and medium brands:** stores that want to add 3D try-on features to increase sales.
   * **Established brands:** businesses that already have a website but want to upgrade with a premium feature.

#### 5. Proposed Solution
The system will let users:
* Create a profile or select a 3D avatar.
* Enter body measurements or use standard body templates.
* Choose a clothing item from the catalog.
* View the item on the avatar from multiple angles.
* Change size or color before checkout.

This makes the shopping process more interactive and more reliable than using only photos.

#### 6. Product Offering and Pricing Model
The platform will be sold in two possible ways:

* **Subscription model:** clients pay monthly or yearly to use the platform and receive updates, maintenance, and support.
* **License / source code model:** clients pay a one-time fee if they want the source code or a private deployment.

This gives flexibility for different types of buyers:
* Smaller businesses can start with a lower monthly payment.
* Larger businesses can choose the source code or custom version for full control.

#### 7. Value Proposition
Our platform is valuable because it creates a direct business benefit:
* More customer trust
* Higher conversion rate
* Lower return rate
* Better brand image
* Stronger competitiveness in online fashion retail

#### 8. Project Scope
The first version will focus on:
* User avatar creation
* Clothing selection and preview
* Basic 3D rendering
* Color and size switching
* Web integration for online store use

#### 9. Development Plan
* **Week 1:** Requirement analysis, UI planning, and feature definition
* **Week 2-3:** 3D simulation development and store integration
* **Week 4:** Testing, improvement, and final presentation

#### 10. Expected Outcome
At the end of the project, we expect to deliver:
* A working 3D clothing simulation prototype
* A clearer online shopping experience
* A strong foundation for future commercialization

#### 11. Funding Justification
If requesting bank support, the proposal should emphasize that this project is not only a technical idea but also a revenue-generating service. The funding will support:
* Software development
* 3D asset creation
* Hosting and deployment
* Testing and maintenance
* Marketing and client onboarding

#### 12. Conclusion
This project provides a practical solution to a real e-commerce problem. It helps shoppers make better purchase decisions and helps businesses sell more with fewer returns. With proper funding and development, the platform can become a valuable commercial product for the online fashion market.
